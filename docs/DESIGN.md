## Brand & Style

The design system projects distinguished academic governance, rigorous institutional competence, and administrative clarity. Crafted specifically for SMVITM Faculty Workload & Timetable Management, it evokes the gravitas of a revered academic institution balanced with modern, precision-engineered timetable analytics.

### Design Movement: Classical Modern Editorial
The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Precision** with **Contemporary Enterprise UI**. It abandons sterile enterprise gray palettes in favor of a warm, archival parchment ambiance paired with deep collegiate tones.

- **Atmosphere**: Authoritative, scholarly, and composed.
- **Visual Mechanics**: Clean tabular structures, high-density informational rhythm, razor-sharp structural rules, and deliberate typographical hierarchy.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Confidence in administrative parity, effortless scheduling resolution, and faculty empowerment.

## Colors

The palette balances historical collegiate weight with specialized functional utility for data visualization and conflict tracking.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Maroon (`#702536`)**: Anchor color applied to major institutional headers, timetable column bands, critical action triggers, and active navigation states.
- **Canvas Cream (`#F5EBDD`)**: The foundational canvas, providing a rich, archival paper glow that mitigates screen fatigue during prolonged administrative reviews.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure, unblemished white used for elevated workload metrics, lecture schedule modules, and dialog overlays.
- **Border Beige (`#E7D9C8`)**: Fine, structural boundary lines defining data matrices, splitters, and container perimeters.

### Accents & Utility
- **Muted Teal (`#167D83`)**: Applied to instructional laboratory sessions, elective course allocations, and workload equilibrium states.
- **Subtle Violet (`#7762A8`)**: Reserved for specialized assignments, postgraduate lectures, inter-departmental duties, and analytical alerts.
- **Structural Text Shade (`#231B1B`)**: An ink-black tinted with warm maroon for all primary textual runs to maintain contrast without harshness.
- **Muted Caption Shade (`#645450`)**: Warm charcoal for secondary metadata, room capacity indicators, and semester tags.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Newsreader** for distinguished display elements with **Manrope** for legible UI controls, backed by **JetBrains Mono** for course IDs, time blocks, and workload calculations.

- **Headlines (Newsreader)**: Delivers academic authority and classical structure across system titles, department designations, and report summaries.
- **Body Text (Manrope)**: High-legibility neutral grotesque handling complex scheduling workflows, dialog inputs, and institutional tables.
- **Labels & Metas (JetBrains Mono)**: Provides precision alignment for room codes (`L-302`), credit hour indicators (`3:0:0`), and timetable slot indicators (`09:00 - 10:00 AM`).

## Layout & Spacing

A structured, data-first grid that supports complex multi-column scheduling layouts while preserving institutional hierarchy.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Structure**: 12-column dynamic grid on desktop, 8-column layout on tablet, and 4-column single-stream on mobile.
- **Timetable Rhythm**: The matrix utilizes a rigid horizontal alignment: time labels lock to a 64px vertical interval, while day columns distribute equally across available width.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop (>= 1280px)`: Full multi-instructor timetable matrix, split workload distribution panels, persistent navigation rail.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1279px)`: Horizontally scrollable timetable matrix with sticky time and day axes; stacked faculty allocation cards.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: Accordion-style daily agenda breakdown, vertical slot stacking, collapse controls for institutional metadata.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface separation rather than diffuse drop shadows, emulating architectural plans and fine print layouts.

### Structural Depth Tiers
- **Flat Surface (`Level 0`)**: The `#F5EBDD` canvas sets the backdrop. Structural containers sit directly on the canvas without elevation.
- **Crisp Plate (`Level 1`)**: White card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px solid `#E7D9C8` boundary. No blur; borders provide structural separation.
- **Interactive Elevation (`Level 2`)**: Hovered schedule blocks and selected class chips employ an offset 2px ambient border tint (`#702536` at 15% opacity) coupled with a micro-offset `0 2px 4px rgba(112, 37, 54, 0.06)`.
- **Administrative Overlays (`Level 3`)**: Timetable conflict resolvers and modal forms use `#FFFFFF` backgrounds bound by a distinct 1px `#702536` structural rim, cast over a 40% opacity `#231B1B` backdrop veil.

## Shapes

The shape system employs disciplined, subtle rounding (`roundedness: 1`), conveying structural integrity and academic poise.

- **Base Corner Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Applied to table cells, schedule chips, input fields, and standard buttons.
- **Large Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Applied to master white card containers, faculty workload dashboards, and modal overlays.
- **Full Radius (Pill)**: Strictly reserved for status tags such as workload allocation counters (e.g., `18/20 Hrs`) and active/inactive markers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Maroon)**: Background `#702536`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px border radius. On hover: `#5A1E2B`.
- **Secondary Action (Teal)**: Background `#167D83`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px border radius. Applied for generation algorithms and report downloads.
- **Outline Control**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E7D9C8`, text `#702536`. Active state shifts border to `#702536`.

### Timetable Grid & Slots
- **Header Blocks**: `#702536` background with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` typography (`Newsreader`, medium weight).
- **Core Lecture Cell**: `#FFFFFF` background, fine `#E7D9C8` right-and-bottom border separators.
- **Laboratory Cell**: Lightened tint of `#167D83` (10% tint on `#FFFFFF`), left accent border (3px solid `#167D83`), typography in `#167D83`.
- **Specialized / Seminar Cell**: Lightened tint of `#7762A8` (10% tint on `#FFFFFF`), left accent border (3px solid `#7762A8`), typography in `#7762A8`.
- **Conflict Cell**: Highlighted in soft Crimson wash (`#FFF0F2`) with a pulsing dotted `#702536` border.

### Cards & Metrics
- **Metric Cards**: Pure white surface, 1px `#E7D9C8` border, 8px radius. Features prominent metric counts in `Newsreader` (`#702536`) with workload units styled in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Input**: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px solid `#E7D9C8`, text `#231B1B`, placeholder `#645450`. Focus state: 1.5px `#702536` solid outline with zero offset blur.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 4px radius on checkboxes, circular on radios. Checked state features `#702536` fill with crisp white iconography.

### Status Indicators & Chips
- **Workload Indicator Pill**: Monospaced font, 12px pill styling. Green tint for optimal load, violet tint (`#7762A8` at 12%) for administrative assignments, and deep maroon for overloaded faculty.
